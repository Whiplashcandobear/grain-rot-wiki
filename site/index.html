What is GRAIN ROT?

GRAIN ROT is a co-op extraction horror builder from Stockholm indie duo Beck & Branch Games (Michel Strömbeck & Nikolai Nyqvist) and publisher Neem Interactive, released on Steam August 7, 2026.

You play a Living Spark — an artificial soul inhabiting a fragile wooden Vessel — descending into procedurally shifting underground ruins to scavenge loot before the Corrupted close in. What you haul back rebuilds your Outpost, and deeper dives bring rarer loot and stranger mutations.
