What Is GRAIN ROT? Game Overview & Platforms

What is GRAIN ROT? A first-person co-op extraction horror builder by Beck & Branch Games on Steam (PC). Tags: survival horror, base building, roguelike.

The game at a glance

GRAIN ROT is a first-person co-op extraction horror builder from Beck & Branch Games, published by Neem Interactive. It launched on Steam on August 7, 2026, after a Next Fest demo that hit 350,000+ downloads.

Genre and platforms

Genres span action, horror, indie, physics, and post-apocalyptic looter. The platform is PC (Steam) only - there is no confirmed console version as of launch. Simplified Chinese is among the 11 supported languages.

System requirements

Per the Steam page, you need Windows 10 64-bit, an Intel i5-4590 or Ryzen 5 2600, 8GB RAM, a GTX 970, DirectX 11, and about 10GB of space. These are modest, which helps the game run on a wide range of laptops.

Price and edition

The Steam list price is $9.99, with a launch-week discount to $8.99. The game is single-purchase; there is no confirmed battle-pass or paid tier as of the manual's research window.
